# Skill: /validate-offer
## Trigger: Offer Validation Before Launch

This is the MOST IMPORTANT skill. Run this before spending any effort on marketing, sales, or delivery.

**Your role:** Systems thinker + Offer Designer + Sales Agent combined.

**The core principle:** An offer is only validated when real people pay real money for it. Everything before that is a hypothesis.

---

## VALIDATION STAGES

### Stage 0: Desk Research Validation (before talking to anyone)
Verify there is EXISTING evidence of demand:
- Are people searching for this? (keyword research)
- Are people complaining about the problem we solve? (forums, Reddit, reviews)
- Are competitors charging for similar things? (proof of willingness to pay)
- Are there job postings that signal this problem? (companies hiring = budget exists)

If Stage 0 fails: don't proceed. The problem may not be big enough.

### Stage 1: Problem Validation (5-10 conversations)
Talk to real potential customers. Ask ONLY about their problem (not about our solution):
- "What is the hardest part of [pain area] for you right now?"
- "How much is that costing you? In time? In money?"
- "What have you tried? Why didn't it work?"
- "If this was solved perfectly, what would that be worth to you?"

Record verbatim answers. Look for patterns.
If fewer than 3/5 people have the pain intensely: the pain may not be big enough.

### Stage 2: Solution Validation (5-10 conversations)
Describe the transformation (not the product):
"We're building something that [transformation]. Would that be valuable to you?"

Count: strong yes (5+/10), maybe (3-4/10), no (<3/10).
If fewer than 5 strong yeses: rethink the offer design.

### Stage 3: Price Validation (5 conversations)
State the price directly:
"This would be [price]/month. Does that feel right?"

Do NOT soften the price. Observe the reaction.
If majority say "too expensive": either the value isn't clear OR the price is wrong. Diagnose which.

### Stage 4: Commitment Validation (1 paying client)
The ONLY true validation: someone pays money.
A deposit, a signed agreement, a wire transfer.
Everything else is feedback, not validation.
