## Trigger: Pricing Research and Model Design

**Your role:** Pricing Strategist Agent (load `/agents/12-pricing-strategist.md` for full methodology).

---

## EXECUTION SEQUENCE

### Step 1: Market Rate Research
Use WebSearch to collect competitor pricing data:

Search queries to run:
- "branding agency pricing 2024 2025"
- "marketing agency retainer cost"
- "[specific service] agency pricing"
- "Clutch top branding agencies pricing"
- "[competitor name] pricing" for each known competitor

For each data point found, record:
- Source URL
- Service type
- Price range
- Model (retainer / project / performance)
- What's included

### Step 2: Value Calculation
For our target ICP, estimate:
- What is the economic value of our core outcome to them?
- What revenue impact does better branding/marketing generate?
- What is the cost of their current problem (lost revenue, wasted spend)?
- What ROI multiple would they expect?

This gives us the CEILING of what we can charge.
Market rates give us the FLOOR we need to beat to be competitive.
Our price should live between ceiling and floor, closer to ceiling.

### Step 3: Price Sensitivity Signals
From existing research, identify:
- What price points cause immediate objections?
- What price points cause "that's reasonable"?
- What price points signal "too cheap" (loss of trust)?

### Step 4: Pricing Model Recommendation
Using the table in Agent 12, recommend which model fits Brand Iconic:
- Productized retainer (recommended for Phase 0-2)
- Or hybrid model with rationale

### Step 5: Tier Design
Design 3 pricing tiers:
- Entry (reduces risk, gets foot in door)
- Core (best value, should be where most clients land)
- Premium (anchor price, makes core look reasonable)

### Step 6: Guarantee Design
Design the specific guarantee language.

Save to: `/research/pricing-research/[YYYY-MM-DD]_pricing-research.md`
