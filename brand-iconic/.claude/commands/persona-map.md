# Skill: /persona-map
## Trigger: Deep Buyer Persona Creation

When this skill is invoked, create a deep buyer persona.

**Your role:** You are the Persona Mapper Agent (load `/agents/02-persona-mapper.md` for full instructions).

**Inputs to collect:**
1. Which customer segment from the market research? (check `/research/market-analysis/`)
2. What job title / role are we mapping?
3. Any existing research or interviews to draw from?

**Execution sequence:**

### Step 1: Research Sources
Search for the persona's voice in the wild:
- Search Reddit: "r/[industry] [problem]" — look for complaint threads
- Search LinkedIn: posts from people with this job title talking about their challenges
- Search G2/Clutch/Trustpilot: reviews of agencies serving this persona
- Search YouTube comments: on videos about their pain points
- Job postings: what are companies with this persona hiring for? (reveals what's breaking)

For each source, copy verbatim quotes into a running document.

### Step 2: Psychology Mapping
Based on research, answer all Level 1-5 questions from `/agents/02-persona-mapper.md`.

### Step 3: Language Bank
Compile at minimum 20 verbatim phrases from real sources.
These phrases MUST be used in all copy targeting this persona.

### Step 4: Journey Map
Map the full awareness journey from problem-unaware to ready-to-buy.

### Step 5: Output
Save to: `/research/persona-maps/[YYYY-MM-DD]_[persona-name].md`
Following exact format in `/agents/02-persona-mapper.md`.

### Step 6: Messaging Implications
At the end of the persona map, write specific recommendations for:
- How to open cold outreach to this persona
- What pain to lead with in copy
- What proof matters most to them
- What objections to pre-empt

**Quality gate:** Minimum 10 real-world data points. No persona based purely on assumptions.
