# WORKFLOW 1: Market Research → Product-Market Fit
## The Foundation. Run This First. Before Everything Else.

---

## WHY THIS WORKFLOW EXISTS

Most agencies fail not because they can't deliver — but because they built an offer nobody wants at a price nobody will pay, positioned in a way that sounds like every other agency.

This workflow eliminates all three of those failure modes through evidence before investment.

**Rule:** We do not write copy for an offer that hasn't been researched.
**Rule:** We do not price anything without market data.
**Rule:** We do not scale outreach before Validation Stage 4 (first paid client).

---

## PHASE 0 EXECUTION PLAN (Weeks 1-3)

### WEEK 1: Intelligence Gathering (Days 1-7)

#### Day 1-2: Segment Identification
Run `/market-research` for 3 candidate ICP segments.

Candidate segments to evaluate:
1. **SaaS startups** (Series A-B, need brand to compete for enterprise clients)
2. **E-commerce brands** ($1M-$10M revenue, need to differentiate as market gets more competitive)
3. **Professional services** (law firms, consultancies, financial advisors — high margin, underbranded)
4. **Real estate / proptech** (high transaction value, image-driven industry)
5. **Health & wellness brands** (crowded, trust-driven, compliance-aware)

Evaluate each against:
- Market size (is it big enough?)
- Pain intensity (how much do they hate their current situation?)
- Ability to pay (do they have budget?)
- Access (can we reach them via LinkedIn/email?)
- Competition (is it dominated by Tier 1 agencies or accessible?)

Select the TOP 2 segments to investigate deeply.

#### Day 3-4: Persona Deep Dive
For each chosen segment, run `/persona-map`.
Target 2 specific job titles per segment.

**Why two titles?** The person who feels the pain (often a Marketing Director) is often different from the person who signs the check (often a CEO or CFO). Know both.

#### Day 5-6: Competitive Intelligence
Run `/competitor-analysis` with a focus on:
- Who is currently serving these two segments?
- What are the negative reviews saying about those agencies?
- What is NOBODY offering that buyers clearly want (based on review complaints)?

#### Day 7: Pricing Research
Run `/pricing-strategy` using data gathered this week.
Goal: Know the floor (freelance rates), the ceiling (top agency rates), and the gap.

---

### WEEK 2: Synthesis + Hypothesis Formation (Days 8-14)

#### Day 8-9: Research Synthesis
Review all research outputs.
Answer 5 critical questions:

1. **Which segment has the most acute, expensive pain?**
   (Intensity × budget = opportunity score)

2. **What does that segment desperately want that nobody is giving them?**
   (This is the positioning gap from Agent 03)

3. **What words do they use that we should borrow verbatim?**
   (From persona language banks)

4. **What price would be both credible to them and profitable for us?**
   (From pricing research — should not feel like guessing)

5. **Who specifically should we talk to first?**
   (10 specific companies/names to contact for validation)

#### Day 10-11: Offer Hypothesis
Run `/offer-design` with all prerequisite research in place.

The first offer should be:
- **Specific** — not "full branding services" but a named, scoped transformation
- **Small enough** to buy without a committee decision
- **Fast enough** to deliver a result in 30 days
- **Profitable** at a price the market will pay
- **Provable** — generates a measurable outcome we can claim

#### Day 12-14: Validation Preparation
Build the validation outreach using `/sales-copy` and `/email-sequence`:
- 10 highly personalized outreach messages (not templates — actually personalized)
- Kickoff validation conversations — goal is learning, not selling
- Define: what does a "strong yes" sound like vs. "polite no"

---

### WEEK 3: Validation Conversations (Days 15-21)

#### Days 15-19: 10 Conversations
Have 10 real conversations with potential buyers.

**The conversation structure (do NOT deviate):**
1. Open: "I'm building something for [segment] and I'd love your honest input. Would you be willing to help me test an idea?"
2. Problem probing: "What's the hardest part of [area] for your business right now?"
3. Impact: "What is that costing you?"
4. History: "What have you tried?"
5. Solution hypothesis: "I'm exploring [transformation]. Is that something you'd find valuable?"
6. Price reality check: "If something like that cost [price]/month, would that be in the range you'd consider?"
7. Commitment test: "If I could show you exactly how we'd do this, would you be open to a proposal?"

Responses to record verbatim in `/research/market-analysis/validation-conversations.md`.

#### Day 20: Synthesis + Pivot Decision

After 10 conversations, decide:

**SCENARIO A: Strong signal (6+ people had intense pain + 3+ wanted a proposal)**
→ Proceed. Finalize offer. Begin outreach at scale.

**SCENARIO B: Mixed signal (3-5 had the pain but objections were consistent)**
→ Iterate. The offer hypothesis needs adjustment. Identify which element is wrong (positioning? price? segment? deliverable?) and adjust.

**SCENARIO C: Weak signal (fewer than 3 felt the pain acutely)**
→ Pivot segment. Return to Week 1 with the next candidate segment. This is not failure — this is the system working. It caught a bad bet early.

#### Day 21: First Proposal (if Scenario A or B+)
Send the first formal proposal to the 2-3 warmest conversations.
Run `/sales-copy` for the proposal narrative.
Use pricing from `/pricing-strategy`.

## PHASE 1 GATE (End of Week 3)

Before advancing to scaled outreach, this checklist must be complete:

```markdown
# Phase 0 → Phase 1 Gate

## Research Complete
- [x] Market research: 2 segments analyzed with HIGH/MEDIUM confidence
- [x] Persona maps: 2 personas with 10+ real data points each
- [x] Competitor analysis: 8+ competitors with positioning gap identified
- [x] Pricing research: market rates documented, pricing tiers designed

## Offer Validated
- [x] Offer hypothesis tested with 10+ conversations
- [x] At least 3 people said "yes, I'd want a proposal"
- [x] Price range confirmed as acceptable to target segment
- [x] Minimum viable version defined

## First Revenue
- [x] At least 1 proposal sent (ideally 2-3)
- [x] Targeting: 1 paying client before scaling outreach

## Infrastructure Ready
- [x] Brand Iconic own positioning defined (/brand-audit on ourselves)
- [x] LinkedIn profile optimized for ICP (done manually — not a skill)
- [x] Outreach templates written and QC'd
- [x] Email sequences ready
- [x] Client folder template ready (/clients/_template/)

PHASE 1 BEGINS WHEN:
→ At least 1 client has signed AND paid a deposit.
Not before.
```

## THE ANTI-PATTERNS THIS WORKFLOW PREVENTS

| Anti-pattern | What it costs | How we prevent it |
|-------------|--------------|-------------------|
| Building an offer in a vacuum | Months of wasted effort | Research gate before offer design |
| Pricing based on "feels right" | Under/overpricing | Market rate + value calculation |
| Targeting everyone | Zero traction everywhere | Segment selection with scoring |
| Scaling bad outreach | Burned list, reputation damage | Validate before scale |
| Copying competitor positioning | Invisible in the market | Gap analysis via Agent 03 |
| Measuring the wrong things | Busy work, no revenue | Analytics framework from day 1 |
